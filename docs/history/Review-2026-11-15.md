## A. Defects

1. **The date was below WCAG AA.** `mix(bg, fg, 0.6)` gave 4.38:1 on the default
   background aeb2c3, 3.35:1 on activeBackground. Now `dimmed()` mixes in steps of
   0.05 from 0.6 until 4.5:1: 3d3e44 and 171b23.
2. **`rgb:` took blanks, signs and 0x.** `strtoul` alone: `rgb:-1/0/0` wrapped into a
   garbage colour where mwc keeps its default. Now hex digits only, as mwc's
   `parse_rgb_form`.
3. **The too long format warning was unreliable.** Beyond 1023 bytes the second
   `strftime` failed too and said nothing, and time and date shared one flag. Now one
   flag each, and a byte in front of the format tells an empty result from one that
   does not fit.
4. **wl_compositor below 4 ended the connection.** `damage_buffer` needs 4. Now 4 is
   required, with a message.
5. **wl_seat below 3 has no `release`.** Now `destroy` there.
6. **The cursor theme went before its buffer.** The old theme was destroyed while
   its buffer was still on the cursor surface; with a failed load the cursor kept a
   destroyed buffer. Now the old theme goes after the new cursor is attached, and a
   failed load keeps the old cursor.
7. **`XCURSOR_SIZE` with `atoi`.** Overflow, also times the scale. Now `strtol`, 1 to
   512, else 24.
8. **A failing `poll` reported "Success".** It printed the Wayland error, which is
   not set then. Now its own errno.
9. **`-v junk` printed the version; `-h` wrote to stderr.** Now a usage error, and the
   usage on stdout for `-h`, as mwc's. `--help` is gone, as in mwc.
