## A. Defects

4. **`px * PANGO_SCALE` overflowed for a huge `CLOCK_FONT`.** Gone with the
   variable: the font is fixed.
5. **The compositor's scale had no limit.** `W * scale`, `H * scale` and the cursor
   size times it overflowed int from a factor near 2^23. Now at most 16, as in
   pulseControl (`buffer_scale`).
6. **The date's descenders were clipped from about 24 pt.** Gone with the fixed 10 pt.
7. **A clock step between reading the clock and arming the timer went unnoticed.**
   The kernel cancels only for a step after `timerfd_settime`; the timer stayed on a
   second far ahead and the clock still for as long as the step. Now the clock is
   read again after arming, and the timer armed anew while it went back, at most 8
   times. Tested with a wrapped `clock_gettime`, as a real step needs CAP_SYS_TIME.
8. **A seat that was already there was not taken when the first one went.** Only the
   first seat announced was bound and no other one kept; until a new one came the
   clock had no keyboard and no pointer. Now up to eight announced seats are kept,
   and when the bound one goes the oldest left is taken. The list is tested in
   test_util; headless mwc has one seat, so the binding itself has no black-box
   test. deskControl, pulseControl and osDisplay have the same code.
9. **`-v` and `-h` exited 0 when stdout failed.** Fully buffered, the write failed at
   exit unnoticed. Now they flush and exit 1 with a message.
